# DinnerSoft — Site Briefing

## Business
DinnerSoft is a Portuguese restaurant management software company that provides an integrated suite of 10 products designed to streamline every aspect of restaurant operations — from front-of-house and kitchen management to staff operations and business intelligence.

Founded by a restaurateur with 15 years of hands-on experience in the industry and owner of 2 restaurants, DinnerSoft was born from the frustration of juggling multiple disconnected systems to run a single restaurant. The platform eliminates that chaos by unifying everything under one ecosystem built specifically for the Portuguese market, with local compliance, language support, and market-specific features.

The target audience is twofold: independent restaurant owners who are drowning in 5+ disconnected tools and losing control of their operations, and new restaurant owners who are overwhelmed by the complexity of running a restaurant for the first time. The tone should be clean, professional, and empathetic — "we've been where you are, and we built the solution." Think modern SaaS with a human touch.

The site follows the StoryBrand framework: the restaurant owner is the hero struggling with operational chaos. DinnerSoft is the guide who understands their pain (empathy from real industry experience) and has the authority (proven solutions, Portuguese compliance) to lead them to clarity and control.

## Pages

- **Home**: StoryBrand funnel homepage. Hero section with headline "Gerir um restaurante não devia ser tão complicado" and two CTAs ("Falar Connosco" + "Experimentar Grátis"). Problem section showing three pain levels: external (5+ disconnected systems), internal (feeling overwhelmed, losing control), philosophical (owners should focus on food and people, not software). Guide section with short founder story (15 years, 2 restaurants, built from lived pain). Plan section with 3-step roadmap (1. Talk to us, 2. We set up your suite, 3. Run your restaurant with clarity). Strong CTA block. Success/stakes section contrasting chaos vs clarity. Product suite grid showing all 10 products grouped by 4 categories (Sala, Cozinha, Equipa, Gestão) with icons and short descriptions linking to individual pages.

- **PicklyMenu**: Product landing page for the Digital Menu System. Pain: outdated paper menus, confused customers, order errors. Solution: interactive digital menus via QR codes and tablets, optional ordering integrated with RestPOS, images and videos for enhanced UX. 3-step plan. Integration callout with RestPOS. CTA: "Experimentar Grátis".

- **RestBooking**: Product landing page for Reservation Management. Pain: missed bookings, double-bookings, phone tag with customers. Solution: comprehensive online table reservation system, real-time availability, automated confirmations. 3-step plan. Integration callout with Manager Station. CTA: "Experimentar Grátis".

- **RestPOS**: Product landing page for Point of Sale System. Pain: slow checkout, compliance headaches, disconnected order flow. Solution: complete POS system handling all orders and transactions, integration with Portuguese licensed systems (ZoneSoft, InvoiceExpress) for official invoice generation. 3-step plan. Integration callout with PicklyMenu and Kitchen Manager. CTA: "Falar Connosco".

- **Kitchen Manager**: Product landing page for Production and Stock Management. Pain: running out of ingredients mid-service, no visibility into production needs, wasted food. Solution: stock management, recipe production tracking, mise en place planning, demand forecasting. 3-step plan. Integration callout with Supply Manager and Chefs Recipes. CTA: "Experimentar Grátis".

- **Supply Manager**: Product landing page for Procurement Manager. Pain: overpaying for supplies, missing promotions, manual price comparison across suppliers. Solution: automated search across all suppliers and promotions for best prices. 3-step plan. Integration callout with Kitchen Manager and Invoice Manager. CTA: "Experimentar Grátis".

- **Chefs Recipes**: Product landing page for Recipe Management. Pain: inconsistent dishes, knowledge locked in one chef's head, no standardization. Solution: recipe discovery and creation based on a library of dish components, standardized preparation. 3-step plan. Integration callout with Kitchen Manager. CTA: "Experimentar Grátis".

- **HR Software**: Product landing page for Staff Management Suite. Pain: scheduling chaos, no time tracking, compliance risks with labor law. Solution: 15 integrated features — time tracking, attendance, employee profiles, vacation management, recruitment, payroll integration, performance management, scheduling and shift planning. Implementation: 43-59 working days for full deployment. 3-step plan. CTA: "Falar Connosco".

- **Invoice Manager**: Product landing page for Financial Control. Pain: boxes of unorganized invoices, no spending visibility, tax season panic. Solution: analyzes, organizes, and stores all restaurant invoices, spending pattern insights, accurate financial records. 3-step plan. Integration callout with Manager Station. CTA: "Experimentar Grátis".

- **Manager Station**: Product landing page for Central Dashboard. Pain: logging into 5 different systems to understand your business, no unified view. Solution: unified management dashboard integrating all DinnerSoft modules, single-login access, overview dashboards across front-of-house, kitchen, administrative, and HR. Links to detailed views in each module. 3-step plan. CTA: "Falar Connosco".

- **Social Manager**: Product landing page for Social Media & Review Management. Pain: no time for social media, missed reviews damaging reputation, inconsistent online presence. Solution: plan and schedule content, generate engaging posts, publish across platforms, monitor and respond to reviews, manage guest feedback. 3-step plan. CTA: "Experimentar Grátis".

- **Sobre Nós (About)**: The founder's story — 15 years in the restaurant industry, owner of 2 restaurants, built DinnerSoft because he lived the pain every day and saw the same struggles in every restaurant around him. Team section with placeholders. Values: built by restaurateurs for restaurateurs, Portuguese market focus, integrated ecosystem vs patchwork of disconnected tools. Mission: give every restaurant owner the tools to focus on what matters — food and people.

## Design Preferences
- Colors: Clean and modern SaaS. Deep blue (#1E3A5F) as primary — trust, professionalism. Vibrant orange (#FF6B35) as accent — energy, CTAs, a subtle nod to restaurant warmth. White (#FFFFFF) backgrounds, light gray (#F8F9FA) for alternating sections, dark (#1A1A2E) for text.
- Typography: Inter for both headings and body — clean, modern, highly readable. Headings bold/semibold, body regular.
- Mood: Professional, clean, trustworthy. Like Stripe or Linear but for restaurants. Lots of whitespace, card-based layouts, subtle shadows, rounded corners (8px). Simple line icons for each product. No stock photos of food — this is a software company. Use abstract illustrations or clean UI mockup placeholders.
- Mobile-responsive throughout.

## Additional Notes
- The site follows the StoryBrand framework by Donald Miller. Every page should reinforce the narrative: the restaurant owner is the hero, DinnerSoft is the guide.
- Two CTAs used contextually: "Falar Connosco" (Talk to Us) for consultative/complex products, "Experimentar Grátis" (Start Free Trial) for self-service products.
- Product pages should always show how the product connects to the broader DinnerSoft ecosystem.
- The mega-dropdown menu for Produtos groups products into 4 categories: Sala (Front-of-House), Cozinha (Kitchen), Equipa (Staff), Gestão (Business).
